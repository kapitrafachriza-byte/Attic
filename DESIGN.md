## Brand & Style

The design system is crafted for a contemporary Indonesian peer-to-peer preloved marketplace where trust, simplicity, and speed are paramount. The brand personality is welcoming, dependable, and effortlessly uncluttered. It caters to urban fashion-forward youth, thrift enthusiasts, and everyday sellers seeking a safe, straightforward way to give pre-owned items a second life. 

The aesthetic is Modern Minimalist Commerce with approachable, tactile accents:
- **Purity and Openness:** Ample negative space, stark white canvases, and airy layout blocks ensure that user-submitted photography remains the focal point without visual distraction.
- **Trust-Forward Touchpoints:** High-contrast deep navy elements create immediate stability and legibility, paired with uplifting, functional accent hues for interaction prompts and safety seals.
- **Approachable Utility:** Soft geometry, intuitive circular navigational anchors ("Cara Jual", "Cara Belanja", "Jaminan Aman"), and pill-shaped action triggers make discovery feel effortless, casual, and safe.

## Colors

The color palette establishes high visual contrast, crystal-clear readability, and strong conversion signaling:

- **Primary Navy (`#0B192C`):** Used for primary conversion points, structural top headers, dominant typography, and solid buttons. It delivers an immediate sense of institutional trust and modern authority.
- **Secondary Vivid Blue (`#1E88E5`):** Serves as the interactive spark for links, active states, badges, and progress steps.
- **Tertiary Safety Green (`#22C55E`):** Reserved for trust cues, verification badges, guaranteed safety indicators ("Aman"), and successful transaction alerts.
- **Neutrals & Surfaces:**
  - Background: Pristine white (`#FFFFFF`) ensures product imagery pops cleanly.
  - Surface Subdued: Very soft gray-blue (`#F8FAFC` to `#F1F5F9`) for search field wells, filter backdrops, and disabled states.
  - Border Strokes: Soft, airy neutral line (`#E2E8F0`) providing definition without heavy division.
  - Body Text: Slate charcoal (`#334155`) ensuring soft reading contrast against stark white backgrounds.

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans is utilized across all levels to preserve unified geometry, geometric clarity, and warm legibility. 

- **Display & Headlines:** Heavy weights (700 to 800) with subtle negative tracking ground hero campaigns and category headers. Titles are punched with direct clarity, mirroring the effortless voice of peer-to-peer commerce.
- **Body & Product Content:** Medium and Regular weights prioritize effortless scanning of prices (Indonesian Rupiah formatting), brand names, and item conditions.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Crisp 600–700 weights ensure chips, interactive navigation steps, filter badges, and navigation links remain clear at small sizes.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column grid system (desktop) that transitions seamlessly into a 4-column layout on mobile devices.

- **Grid & Alignment:**
  - Desktop: 12-column layout, max-width of 1240px, centered with `margin-desktop` (2.5rem) and `gutter-desktop` (1.5rem).
  - Tablet: 8-column layout with 1.25rem gutters and 1.5rem margins.
  - Mobile: 4-column fluid layout with 1rem margins and 0.75rem to 1rem gutters, optimizing dual-column product cards.
- **Product Card Grids:** 
  - Standard product catalogs render in 2-column configurations on mobile, expanding to 4-column or 5-column configurations on wider viewports.
- **Rhythm & Padding:**
  - Micro spaces (`space-xs`, `space-sm`) drive internal card alignments and tag padding.
  - Section blocks and hero banners utilize `space-xl` for breathing room between promotional carousels, action steps, and item lists.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system favors light, clean flatness paired with delicate ambient lift to maintain a crisp, hygienic marketplace feel:

- **Level 0 (Flat):** Standard base cards, list items, and input wells utilize clean borders (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) over flat white backgrounds without drop shadows.
- **Level 1 (Card Rest):** Interactive product cards, category circular icons, and floating bottom sheets sit on a delicate, ambient blur: `box-shadow: 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.05), 0 1px 4px -1px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Active Overlays):** Triggered when hovering over clickable cards, active search autocomplete dropdowns, and sticky headers: `box-shadow: 0 10px 25px -5px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.08), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.04)`.
- **Hero & Media Depth:** Photographic content carries gentle inner edge treatments and soft rounded masking rather than artificial heavy dropshadows.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a friendly, modern rounded language (`roundedness: 2`):

- **Component Standard (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** Used for input fields, filter dropdowns, badges, and smaller listing containers.
- **Cards & Promos (`1rem` / `16px`):** Applied to product cards, banner images, and modal containers.
- **Large Banners & Floating Surfaces (`1.5rem` / `24px`):** Applied to prominent hero sections and sticky bottom interaction bars.
- **Full Radius (Pill & Circular Shapes):**
  - Buttons (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) provide inviting touch affordances.
  - Informational steps ("Cara Jual", "Cara Belanja", "Proses Pesanan") use strict 1:1 circle containers with a centered icon or illustration and dynamic border rings.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), `#0B192C` solid fill, white text, bold font weight. Height: 44px (desktop) / 40px (compact). Hover state: `#1E293B`.
- **Secondary / Action Button:** Pill-shaped, crisp white background with `#0B192C` text and 1px border (`#E2E8F0` or `#0B192C`). Hover transitions smoothly to subtle `#F8FAFC`.
- **Ghost / Translucent Overlay Button:** As seen in hero banners, semi-transparent black or white backdrop (`rgba(0,0,0,0.4)` or `rgba(255,255,255,0.85)`) with pill outline for secondary exploration.

### Circular Step Icons ("Cara Pakai" Section)
- **Container:** Uniform circles (68px to 80px diameter) centered above title labels.
- **Border Treatment:** Distinctive 2.5px solid border outline colored with vibrant lime/green (`#22C55E`) or accent blue (`#1E88E5`), framing an inner photo or graphic.
- **Typography & Interaction:** Accompanied below by concise body-md/body-sm text labels centered beneath each circle. Clicking directs to step-by-step onboarding walkthroughs.

### Search Field
- **Structure:** Pill-shaped or soft-rounded (`rounded-full` or `rounded-lg`) wide input container with a gentle neutral fill (`#F1F5F9`).
- **Iconography:** Magnifying glass icon placed at the prefix position (`#94A3B8`). Placeholder text styled in neutral tone (`#94A3B8`).

### Product Cards
- **Structure:** Top image container (`aspect-ratio: 1/1` or `4/5`) with `rounded-lg` corners, followed by minimal vertical text padding below.
- **Details Hierarchy:**
  1. Product Name: Single or dual-line truncated, regular body text.
  2. Price: Bold, pronounced Primary Navy (`#0B192C`).
  3. Condition & Location Tag: Subdued micro-labels (`label-xs`) in `#64748B`.
- **Quick Action ("Nego" / Like):** Floating heart icon top-right with soft translucent backing.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- **Style:** Compact pill shapes with 32px height, subtle `1px solid #E2E8F0` border, `#F8FAFC` background. Active state toggles to solid `#0B192C` fill with white text.

### Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Input Fields:** Soft rounded rectangles (`rounded-lg`), subtle neutral border (`#CBD5E1`), focus ring tinted with Secondary Blue (`#1E88E5`) at 3px blur offset.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Curved corner square / full circle with primary navy checkmarks when selected.
